00. Executive decision and controlled-document precedence
The Mathmag Builders Management System (MBMS) contains seven CORE MODULES. Modules 1-6 are the 
client/project delivery lifecycle. Module 7 - Central Administration - is cross-cutting and operates continuously across 
them. Client, employee, subcontractor, supplier and management dashboards are role-specific portals within this 
single platform, not extra business modules.
This V3 document and the corresponding module V3 addenda are the newest governance/integration layer. They 
preserve previously approved module requirements except where an older generic or inconsistent statement must 
be interpreted using the later owner vs GM, delegated approval, independent review, statutory/safety and audit 
policies. Legal compliance and monetary delegation limits need verification and configuration; no software 
implementation is claimed.

02.1 Delegation and approval interpretation
 Every power has authority_category, role/person grant, project scope, value threshold, risk category, effective 
dates, evidence requirement and substitution/escalation route.
 “Management” in existing Module 1-6 documents means a valid delegated manager for ordinary decisions and 
the GM for above-limit or expressly reserved operational approvals; it never implicitly means owner approval.
 The owner sees protected strategic/audit risk summaries but remains outside routine finance, supplier, 
attendance, project deadlines and contract-related operational queues unless the matter is expressly owner-
reserved.
 Mandatory technical, client, legal, HR and finance review steps are not optional just because a GM has spending 
authority.
 PM responsibility to coordinate is distinct from authority to contract, sign, make payment, change material 
ownership, certify a structural drawing or approve an independent investigation.
 GM absence: HR activates a time-limited preapproved acting deputy according to standing approved succession; 
if no deputy is available, preapproved emergency management committee operates via limited quorum and 
recorded votes, never exercising owner-reserved powers.

05.1 System-wide state envelope and integrity
 Every workflow action stores id, parent/project/engagement, type, action, actorUserId, representedRole, 
authorizingMandateId, actionTimestamp, previousState, nextState, reasonCode, attachments, correlationId and 
source revisions.
 Business state history and evidence attachments must be tamper-evident and non-deletable by ordinary admins. 
Personally identifying records need legally valid retention and access safeguards; immutable audit does not 
mean unrestricted public disclosure.
 File versions use content hash, classification, owner, documentPurpose, supersedesVersionId, approvedBy, 
approvedAt and exact client publication grant. Never expose storage URL without server-side authorization.
 Critical operation endpoints require MFA or step-up verification, replay/idempotency key, server-side authority 
check and transaction-level segregation of duties. Exceptions reference applicable policy rule.
 Client-facing or worker-facing notifications are async, retriable and logged; original write transaction is 
authoritative even if a notification fails.

07. Security, audit and operational resilience
 Protected audit channel routes allegations involving the GM or a department head to independent auditor and 
nonconflicted owner/governance decision-maker; access logs also audited.
 HR, auditor and customer data have partitioned confidentiality; least privilege applies at tenant, project, case, 
record type and individual file-level.
 No transaction creator can approve all stages, no payable payee-change to unverified destination; no duplicate 
invoices/payroll periods, no hidden versions/stock balance edits.
 Encrypted transport, encryption at rest, segregated key/secrets administration, backups, restore drills, retention 
schedule and offboarding revocation.
 Offline site/mobile sync must display upload acknowledgment, preserve original local client submission id, 
prevent duplicates and never label not-yet-synced evidence as completed.
 Alerts escalate based on project risk/severity/threshold; routine site operations continue while only affected 
unsafe assets/payments/work are restricted.

10. Developer acceptance criteria (cross-module)
 MAST-UAT-01. Customer intake cannot create active project without agreed site visit date.
 MAST-UAT-02. Only a valid 2D acceptance enables 3D.
 MAST-UAT-03. No engineering handover unless predecessor technically approved.
 MAST-UAT-04. Client may not retrieve working editable CAD/ArchiCAD design files.
 MAST-UAT-05. Client acceptance binds exact published proposal and financial revision.
 MAST-UAT-06. Accounts manually initiates customer overdue reminders.
 MAST-UAT-07. An authorized financial milestone exception does not change receivable ledger.
 MAST-UAT-08. No professional stamp from unverified or expired qualified identity.
 MAST-UAT-09. M4 conditional completion cannot remove construction NCA statutory lock.
 MAST-UAT-10. Client service selection is not an executed contract.
 MAST-UAT-11. Company signature requires approved final version and valid signatory authority.
 MAST-UAT-12. Module 6 can open planning-only while physical execution is blocked.
 MAST-UAT-13. Worker original evidence is instantly visible to PM/GM without supervisor gate.
 MAST-UAT-14. Supervisor disagreement appends review and preserves original media.
 MAST-UAT-15. Unrelated worker cannot see another worker compensation.
 MAST-UAT-16. Supplier bank account change automatically holds affected pending payment.
 MAST-UAT-17. Three-way match against accepted GRN quantity prevents duplicate payment.
 MAST-UAT-18. Stock transit remains in transit until receiving Storekeeper independently accepts.
 MAST-UAT-19. Client-owned stock movement blocked absent ownership entitlement/required client consent.
 MAST-UAT-20. Equipment cannot be assigned to unqualified/expired operator.
 MAST-UAT-21. Critical pre-use defect restricts affected equipment only.
 MAST-UAT-22. Repairer cannot self-certify independent return-to-service safety clearance.
 MAST-UAT-23. Fuel issue has equipment/site/meter/operator/custody evidence.
 MAST-UAT-24. Serious stock losses trigger fair investigation; no automatic salary deduction.
 MAST-UAT-25. A chargeable variation needs management-approved client accepted exact scope.
 MAST-UAT-26. PM cannot publish confidential worker disputes to client portal.
 MAST-UAT-27. Owner cannot bypass GM operational approvals except reserved governance powers.
 MAST-UAT-28. GM-linked whistleblower case bypasses conflicted GM controls.

1. Implementation interpretation and source preservation
This document intentionally retains the complete existing revised module specification after this amendment.
Do not erase earlier workflow, screenshots, forms, stages or user-approved protections. Configure the shared
functions from the V3 Master Architecture and Module 7 specification. The addendum is not permission to skip
work or approvals.
2. Shared mandatory company controls
 • GOV-01 Governance: The owner retains reserved strategic powers and protected oversight, while the
 employed General Manager (GM) holds company-wide operational accountability. Owner access to reporting
does not authorize routine operational interference.
• GOV-02 Delegation: Department heads, Project Managers and designated officers may authorize decisions
 only within documented project, function, category, value and risk limits; above-limit or exceptional decisions
go to GM. No employee may approve their own originating action.
• GOV-03 Technical independence: Valid safety and professional certifications are performed by competent
 authorized persons; neither a GM nor a financial waiver can substitute for a mandatory technical/statutory
clearance.
• GOV-04 Evidence: Worker, junior designer, client, storekeeper, inspector, supplier and auditor original
 submissions are preserved with authorship, timestamp, linked versions and tamper-evident history. Review
adds new records; it does not overwrite originals.
• GOV-05 Segregation: Requests, verification, approval, payment, receipt and audit must be appropriately
 independent. Where team size makes perfect separation impractical, a documented compensating review by
an independent qualified officer is mandatory.
• GOV-06 Client access: Client portal content is limited to that client/project and expressly published revision;
 source design files, worker complaints, confidential pay, internal contracts and audit cases remain restricted.

3. Updated operating workflow and stage gates
Budget approval
Lead QS approves technical estimate; delegated management separately authorizes client release of summary
and permissioned detailed budget.
Client acceptance
Client portal sign-off or uploaded signed paper acceptance of exact proposal version, with evidence and identity.
Verification
Delegated management independently verifies client acceptance and signed scope; unauthorized acceptance
never advances stage.
Finance
Accounts independently verifies actual received net payments and milestone conditions from reconciled
records; reminders only manually dispatched by Accounts.
Release
Management formally authorizes Module 4 once acceptance and financial gate pass; properly documented
financial exception permitted, debt remains outstanding.

04 Proposal snapshot and cost presentation
• Build a read-only proposal snapshot referencing approved drawing revision IDs, approved QS estimate
revision, scope inclusions/exclusions and document manifest.
• Generate client proposal summary plus supporting documents; keep the detailed QS budget internally unless
management explicitly allows the client a detailed view.
• Store separate timestamps for upload, technical approval, management publication, client access,
acceptance and subsequent supersession.
• Provide clear version label on client review screens and sign-off forms so accepted documents cannot be
confused with newer alternatives.
• Prevent direct file download through guessed URLs, cached links or copied share links after publication
access is revoked.
• Retain historical proposal versions after revision; do not erase a version accepted by the client.
05 Client review and acceptance routes
Portal route
• Authenticated client views authorized PDFs/images and the approved pricing view, submits comments and
explicitly signs/accepts the exact package.
• Record authenticated identity, acting capacity, acceptance wording, date/time, version hash and acceptance
receipt.
Meeting and signed-form route
• Staff records physical/virtual review meeting, version actually presented, comments and minutes; meetings
alone do not imply acceptance.
• Upload signed acceptance form with identity and signature evidence, identify exact proposal revision and
assign verification to management.
Management verification
• Check client authority, file/scope match, accepted costs, any qualifications, outstanding design corrections
and authenticity of evidence.
• Statuses include Pending, Verified, Returned for Clarification and Rejected, each with recorded reasons.

07 Accounts and payment milestone controls
• Accounts verifies incoming payments against reliable channel evidence, project fee category and unique
transaction reference; duplicate reference checks required.
• Receipts and statements may be published to the client portal and optionally shared via configured
WhatsApp/email channels.
• Timing of financial reminders is manually controlled by Accounts (confirmed policy); the system may assist
with drafts/queues but must not send at a hard-coded automatic cadence.
• Module 4 financial gate tests the specific agreed planning/approval service milestone, not necessarily the
entire overall design/build contract price.
• Only management can authorize a permitted payment exception, with reason, scope, conditions and
timestamp. Such exception never overrides mandatory statutory requirements.
• Part-payments, overpayments, reversals, refunds and reallocations require traceable financial adjustments
rather than edits to historical receipt rows.
08 Contract and service pathway integration
• Module 3 acceptance is acceptance of a defined plan/budget proposal; it is not automatically a construction
contract or authority for mobilization.
• Clients who select drawings/printing only or own-builder design services can terminate the engagement at
their contracted milestone without being forced into full approval/build flows.
• Later service add-ons (approvals, inspection, supervision, labour or full construction) must open controlled
commercial requests in Module 5, approved by management.
• If detailed budgets are restricted, the accepted proposal should still clearly show the client-facing financial
scope and obligations they are agreeing to.

10 Shared rules carried across all six modules
 CROSS-MODULE RULE
IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENT
One lifecycle record
Maintain one project ID from confirmed site-visit booking
through design, approvals, contracts, construction and
post-handover. Never create a disconnected project just to
open a new module.
Role separation
User role, project assignment and explicit approval authority
determine every permitted action. Technical sign-off, financial
validation, management approval and client acceptance are
distinct records.
Evidence integrity
Preserve original submissions, attachments, timestamps,
authors and immutable decision histories. Corrections create
new records or versions; no silent overwriting.
Selective client access
Client accounts can access only their own projects and
explicitly authorized files or updates. Internal working/source
files, staff performance data and financial notes are never
published by default.
Conditional service scope
Clients may purchase design only, printing only, approvals,
technical supervision, labour-only building or full construction.
Modules not contracted are not mandatory project steps.
Management-controlled exceptions
Any permitted exception requires identified management
authorization, reason, date and retained evidence. Mandatory
legal/technical clearances cannot be overridden.
Security and audit
Require authenticated accounts, least privilege, encrypted
transport/storage, file scanning, access logs, project-scoped
permissions, backups and recoverability.

14 User acceptance tests and release criteria
• TEST 01 - Reject proposal publication without Lead QS approved budget and positive management
publication authorization.
• TEST 02 - Allow management to publish summary budget while detailed budget stays inaccessible to client.
• TEST 03 - Accept both secure portal and client-signed uploaded acceptance, and reject mismatch to proposal
version.
• TEST 04 - Management verification must exist for every acceptance, including signed-form route.
• TEST 05 - Block Module 4 release on unpaid milestone unless permitted management exception is properly
recorded.
• TEST 06 - Accounts can issue receipts/statements but cannot approve financial exceptions.
• TEST 07 - Reminders do not auto-send at a hard-coded frequency; Accounts explicitly schedules/sends them.
• TEST 08 - Accepted proposal is immutable after change; revisions create new client acceptance route where
required.

2. Shared mandatory company controls
 • GOV-01 Governance: The owner retains reserved strategic powers and protected oversight, while the
 employed General Manager (GM) holds company-wide operational accountability. Owner access to reporting
does not authorize routine operational interference.
• GOV-02 Delegation: Department heads, Project Managers and designated officers may authorize decisions
 only within documented project, function, category, value and risk limits; above-limit or exceptional decisions
go to GM. No employee may approve their own originating action.
• GOV-03 Technical independence: Valid safety and professional certifications are performed by competent
 authorized persons; neither a GM nor a financial waiver can substitute for a mandatory technical/statutory
clearance.
• GOV-04 Evidence: Worker, junior designer, client, storekeeper, inspector, supplier and auditor original
 submissions are preserved with authorship, timestamp, linked versions and tamper-evident history. Review
adds new records; it does not overwrite originals.
• GOV-05 Segregation: Requests, verification, approval, payment, receipt and audit must be appropriately
 independent. Where team size makes perfect separation impractical, a documented compensating review by
an independent qualified officer is mandatory.
• GOV-06 Client access: Client portal content is limited to that client/project and expressly published revision;
 source design files, worker complaints, confidential pay, internal contracts and audit cases remain restricted.

04 Enquiry and booking capture
Channel-aware client relations
• Unify message reference, source channel, date/time, client contacts, client interests and follow-up notes;
avoid copying conversations without appropriate integration access.
• Do not mark every inquiry as a pipeline project. Maintain separate Inbox Lead and Confirmed Visit / Project
statuses.
• AI-supported enquiry triage and handoff to humans is an optional phased feature, not a prerequisite for
Module 1 completion.
Site visit booking form
• Capture client / legal owner, telephone, email, property location, county, approximate plot size, requested
development, agreed appointment date/time and visiting officer.
• System assigns one immutable project reference only when appointment has been jointly agreed. Rebooking
changes the appointment and retains its history.
• Invite client to portal when appropriate; absence of a portal account must not block field visit.
05 Mobile site-data collection and survey integrity
 SECTION
REQUIRED FIELDS / BEHAVIOUR
A. Visit identity
Project ID; client; visit date; visiting officer; location/address;
geographic map pin; GPS accuracy if present.
B. Site geometry
Measured plot dimensions, approximate boundaries, access
widths, existing structures, property features, plot sketch and
measurement confidence.
C. Physical conditions
Terrain, slope, drainage/flood cues, soil observations stated as
preliminary, vegetation, adjacent plots, utilities and access.
D. Environment
Observed wind direction, sunlight/sunrise/sunset orientation,
likely shading and source/assumptions of observations.
E. Client requirements
Bedrooms/use cases, storeys, aesthetic preferences, budget
aspirations, development phasing, accessibility needs and key
constraints.
F. Media
Field photos/videos with captions, category, capture time,
upload attribution, file hash, project and access classification.
G. Follow-up
Unknown measurements, missing title/survey data, required
investigations and questions for client/architect.
MANDATORY CONTROL
Recorded soil observations or field measurements are preliminary and must not be presented as a certified
geotechnical, boundary or structural survey unless separately commissioned and verified.

06 Site report generation and review
• Generate a versioned Site Visit & Design Brief Report incorporating selected client info, location, dimensions,
terrain, orientation, client needs, limitations, site sketches and photo captions.
• Produce a staff-review PDF and editable document if supported; final client publication uses a locked
PDF/image version. Source and editable files remain internal.
• Reports have Draft, Submitted, Returned, Approved, Superseded and Published flags as distinct concepts.
• Lead Architect comments must link to specific report version, required correction, owner and review
timestamp.
• Publication control selects exact report version and individual media assets; client visibility may be revoked
with a logged reason without destroying the internal original.
• Management can independently inspect original field uploads and the lead review trail, reflecting the
evidence-transparency principle introduced later in Module 6.
07 Module 2 integration and project service pathways
• Handoff artifact contains the approved site report, design brief, photo/sketch set, location and a designated
junior architect.
• Lead Architect and assigned junior can access internal working resources; client portal receives only
approved PDFs/images.
• Design-only, approval-inclusive, full build, labour-only and supervision clients share the initial ID, but their
later module activation depends on contracted service scope.
• Follow-up site visits become additional visit records linked to the same project; never create a new project for
the same engagement by default.
• If new site findings materially change the design brief after 2D approval, open a controlled cross-module
revision and revalidate dependent outputs.

08 Shared rules carried across all six modules
 CROSS-MODULE RULE
IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENT
One lifecycle record
Maintain one project ID from confirmed site-visit booking
through design, approvals, contracts, construction and
post-handover. Never create a disconnected project just to
open a new module.
Role separation
User role, project assignment and explicit approval authority
determine every permitted action. Technical sign-off, financial
validation, management approval and client acceptance are
distinct records.
Evidence integrity
Preserve original submissions, attachments, timestamps,
authors and immutable decision histories. Corrections create
new records or versions; no silent overwriting.
Selective client access
Client accounts can access only their own projects and
explicitly authorized files or updates. Internal working/source
files, staff performance data and financial notes are never
published by default.
Conditional service scope
Clients may purchase design only, printing only, approvals,
technical supervision, labour-only building or full construction.
Modules not contracted are not mandatory project steps.
Management-controlled exceptions
Any permitted exception requires identified management
authorization, reason, date and retained evidence. Mandatory
legal/technical clearances cannot be overridden.
Security and audit
Require authenticated accounts, least privilege, encrypted
transport/storage, file scanning, access logs, project-scoped
permissions, backups and recoverability.

1. Implementation interpretation and source preservation
This document intentionally retains the complete existing revised module specification after this amendment.
Do not erase earlier workflow, screenshots, forms, stages or user-approved protections. Configure the shared
functions from the V3 Master Architecture and Module 7 specification. The addendum is not permission to skip
work or approvals.
2. Shared mandatory company controls
 • GOV-01 Governance: The owner retains reserved strategic powers and protected oversight, while the
 employed General Manager (GM) holds company-wide operational accountability. Owner access to reporting
does not authorize routine operational interference.
• GOV-02 Delegation: Department heads, Project Managers and designated officers may authorize decisions
 only within documented project, function, category, value and risk limits; above-limit or exceptional decisions
go to GM. No employee may approve their own originating action.
• GOV-03 Technical independence: Valid safety and professional certifications are performed by competent
 authorized persons; neither a GM nor a financial waiver can substitute for a mandatory technical/statutory
clearance.
• GOV-04 Evidence: Worker, junior designer, client, storekeeper, inspector, supplier and auditor original
 submissions are preserved with authorship, timestamp, linked versions and tamper-evident history. Review
adds new records; it does not overwrite originals.
• GOV-05 Segregation: Requests, verification, approval, payment, receipt and audit must be appropriately
 independent. Where team size makes perfect separation impractical, a documented compensating review by
an independent qualified officer is mandatory.
• GOV-06 Client access: Client portal content is limited to that client/project and expressly published revision;
 source design files, worker complaints, confidential pay, internal contracts and audit cases remain restricted.

04 Application applicability matrix
• Assess County Government approvals and any relevant environmental, construction registration or other
applicable authority requirements on a project-by-project basis.
• Store status as Not Applicable, Required-Not Started, In Preparation, Submitted, Corrections Requested,
Approved, Rejected or Deferred with documented reason.
• Do not assume every project needs every listed approval, and do not assert legal exemption without
appropriately recorded project-specific assessment.
• Planning applications can run in parallel when lawfully permitted and their inputs are ready; dependent
actions remain blocked until prerequisites are satisfied.
• Contractor-dependent NCA project registration is a separate obligation that may be pending while relevant
planning approvals are complete.
• A client who buys design-only or printed documents may have no Module 4 application workflow; mark
skipped by service scope rather than artificially complete.

06 Government application and payment management
Application files
• Create authority-specific case with applicant/owner, property parcel/county, relevant professionals,
application type, current package and tracking/reference number.
• Upload official submission receipt, payment voucher, authority portal acknowledgement, permit/approval and
correspondence as immutable evidence.
• Keep authority decision chronology and all fee demands; date future follow-ups rather than relying on
memory.
Two permitted statutory fee routes
• Direct client payment: record invoice/instructions, client-uploaded proof, authority confirmation and financial
responsibility.
• Mathmag-coordinated payment: Approvals Officer initiates fee request, authorized management expenditure
gate applies, Accounts disburses and verifies receipts.
• Never mark a fee paid solely because an upload exists; verify against authority or trusted payment evidence
when available.

08 Conditional completion, NCA dependency and Module
5/6 gates
• Module 4 can reach Planning Approvals Complete / Contractor Registration Pending when applicable
design/planning permits are available but contractor details are not yet finalized.
• Manage NCA project registration and contractor appointment information as a separate compliance work
item triggered by later execution of the applicable contract.
• Project Manager may open Module 6 preconstruction planning after an executed construction agreement, but
physical mobilization remains locked until all required registration, approvals, financial gates, readiness
assessment and management release.
• For design-only clients or clients appointing another contractor, approvals and contractor-registration
responsibility must be documented accurately in the relevant engagement; Mathmag cannot override
requirements placed on the responsible parties.
• Management authorizes formal publication to the client portal after internal approval evidence has been
checked.
• Any late authority corrections that affect client-accepted scope, cost, programme or construction works must
trigger controlled cross-module impact checks.

09 Controlled release to client portal
• Approvals Officer uploads approved documents to internal case record with issuing authority, reference,
date, stamped version and validation evidence.
• Management sees preview, manifest and list of outstanding approvals before authorizing exact file-level
publication.
• Client portal shows explicit status and disclaimers for pending obligations; never show NCA project
registration as approved when still open.
• Publication can be revoked or superseded only through traceable actions, not deletion of evidence.
• Professional and county documents retain their authentic original appearance and may not be recreated as
simulated official permits.
10 Shared rules carried across all six modules
 CROSS-MODULE RULE
IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENT
One lifecycle record
Maintain one project ID from confirmed site-visit booking
through design, approvals, contracts, construction and
post-handover. Never create a disconnected project just to
open a new module.
Role separation
User role, project assignment and explicit approval authority
determine every permitted action. Technical sign-off, financial
validation, management approval and client acceptance are
distinct records.
Evidence integrity
Preserve original submissions, attachments, timestamps,
authors and immutable decision histories. Corrections create
new records or versions; no silent overwriting.
Selective client access
Client accounts can access only their own projects and
explicitly authorized files or updates. Internal working/source
files, staff performance data and financial notes are never
published by default.
Conditional service scope
Clients may purchase design only, printing only, approvals,
technical supervision, labour-only building or full construction.
Modules not contracted are not mandatory project steps.
Management-controlled exceptions
Any permitted exception requires identified management
authorization, reason, date and retained evidence. Mandatory
legal/technical clearances cannot be overridden.
Security and audit
Require authenticated accounts, least privilege, encrypted
transport/storage, file scanning, access logs, project-scoped
permissions, backups and recoverability.

14 User acceptance tests and release criteria
• TEST 01 - Approvals Officer cannot add simulated professional certification or publish client documents
directly.
• TEST 02 - Client-pay and Mathmag-pay statutory fee pathways keep evidence and accounts states distinct.
• TEST 03 - Authority correction returns to the correct department head, preserves original approved versions
and triggers cost impact checks.
• TEST 04 - Conditional Module 4 completion can coexist with pending contractor-specific NCA registration.
• TEST 05 - Module 6 physical mobilization cannot occur simply because county planning approvals are
complete.
• TEST 06 - Print-only/design-only client can exit without being forced into authority applications.
• TEST 07 - Management must authorize every published final approval document.
• TEST 08 - Resubmission has new manifest/reference and retains prior authority responses.

1. Implementation interpretation and source preservation
This document intentionally retains the complete existing revised module specification after this amendment.

2. Shared mandatory company controls
 • GOV-01 Governance: The owner retains reserved strategic powers and protected oversight, while the
 employed General Manager (GM) holds company-wide operational accountability. Owner access to reporting
does not authorize routine operational interference.
• GOV-02 Delegation: Department heads, Project Managers and designated officers may authorize decisions
 only within documented project, function, category, value and risk limits; above-limit or exceptional decisions
go to GM. No employee may approve their own originating action.
• GOV-03 Technical independence: Valid safety and professional certifications are performed by competent
 authorized persons; neither a GM nor a financial waiver can substitute for a mandatory technical/statutory
clearance.
• GOV-04 Evidence: Worker, junior designer, client, storekeeper, inspector, supplier and auditor original
 submissions are preserved with authorship, timestamp, linked versions and tamper-evident history. Review
adds new records; it does not overwrite originals.
• GOV-05 Segregation: Requests, verification, approval, payment, receipt and audit must be appropriately
 independent. Where team size makes perfect separation impractical, a documented compensating review by
an independent qualified officer is mandatory.
• GOV-06 Client access: Client portal content is limited to that client/project and expressly published revision;
 source design files, worker complaints, confidential pay, internal contracts and audit cases remain restricted.

09 Individual worker onboarding and reporting channels
• Every directly engaged skilled worker has a unique personal account at onboarding; skilled workers
employed by appointed subcontractors also receive unique accounts linked to their subcontractor employer
and project.
• Worker profile includes contact, trade/competence, permitted projects, employment relationship,
supervisor/PM, safety induction/PPE, assigned tasks, pay arrangement only if directly employed, and activation
dates.
• Original daily work report captures assigned task, self-reported quantities, description, date/time and
photos/videos; immediately visible directly to PM and management.
• A worker submission is not the same as verified work. Supervisors append independent verification status
(confirmed, partial, disputed or pending) and reasons without editing or suppressing the submission.
• Mobile-first, practical low-bandwidth media uploads with draft/offline queuing, compression and confirmation
of successful sync are recommended.
• Worker may contest verification through portal. PM investigates; management sees disputes independently
and may intervene with a reasoned logged decision.
• Subcontractor representative has a restricted dashboard of their authorized team and project; individual
worker accounts do not create an automatic direct Mathmag payroll/employment relationship.
MANDATORY CONTROL
There must be no "supervisor approval before management visibility" queue. Worker media and descriptions
enter the central record immediately and remain unaltered.

21 Safety hazards, incident reporting and work
resumption
Independent hazard and incident channel
• All workers can independently report hazards, near misses, incidents and injuries with photos/videos; original
report directly reaches PM and management without supervisor approval.
• Supervisor takes immediate protective actions, including halting unsafe activity and requesting emergency
help. Critical cases generate immediate management escalation.
• PM investigates, records contributing factors and tracks corrective actions, with competent safety/technical
personnel and statutory notifications where required.
Release after hazard correction
• Supervisor independently checks corrective actions and records evidence; PM may authorize routine
resumption.
• Serious events require management authorization and all applicable competent professional or regulator
clearance before affected activity resumes.
• Retain safety hold, inspection outcome, clearance certificates, manager decision and date. Worker reporting
must not trigger retaliation.
22 Safety induction, toolbox talks and PPE
Induction and talks
• Supervisor conducts site induction and toolbox briefings; logs topic, hazards, instructions, trainer, date and
attendees.
• Every worker independently acknowledges attendance through own portal, immediately visible to
PM/management; acknowledgments do not substitute for competency checks.

29 Priority response, verified closure and no-response rule
Response-time priority
• Classify claims Critical, Urgent or Routine. Management configures separate initial-response and resolution
target intervals consistent with contract, severity and legal obligations.
• System timestamps claim, acknowledgment, inspection, response, resolution; escalates
approaching/overdue milestones to PM and management.
• Critical safety hazards receive immediate protective or emergency action regardless of liability and ordinary
business approvals.
Client confirmation and dispute
• PM verifies repairs and obtains mandatory technical clearances; client reviews evidence and explicitly
confirms satisfaction or disputes findings in portal.
• Disputed claims remain under investigation with independent management oversight; client-confirmed and
management-administrative closures have distinct statuses.
Client non-response
• System sends reminders at management-set intervals and logs delivery/response. PM compiles completed
repair evidence and communication history.
• Management alone may authorize a reasoned administrative closure if contract and law permit. Client silence
is never satisfaction or release of rights.
30 Reopening claims, expiry tracking and final warranty
close-out
Reopening
• Client can request reopening of a previous claim with new evidence; PM reviews original history and
investigates. Management sees all requests and can intervene in contested decisions.
• Original complaints, prior repairs and closures are retained. Reopening does not automatically prove
eligibility for free work or extinguish applicable rights.
Expiry monitoring
• Maintain contract-specific warranty and defects-liability start/end dates for overall building and separately for
trades, suppliers, components and subcontractors if applicable.
• System calculates due dates, sends reminders at management-defined intervals and alerts management of
approaching/passed expiry and outstanding claims.
• Expiry never erases, auto-closes or prejudges unresolved/reopened claims or statutory/contractual duties.
Final administrative close-out
• Project Manager prepares a final report: expiry periods, original/reopened claims, repairs, client
confirmations/disputes, administrative no-response closures, outstanding defects, clearances and continuing
obligations.
• Management independently reviews and alone authorizes administrative close-out when appropriate. The
system must not label it "all liabilities discharged".

31 Shared rules carried across all six modules

IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENT
One lifecycle record

Preserve original submissions, attachments, timestamps,

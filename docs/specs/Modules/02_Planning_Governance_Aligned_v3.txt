1. Implementation interpretation and source preservation
This document intentionally retains the complete existing revised module specification after this amendment.
Do not erase earlier workflow, screenshots, forms, stages or user-approved protections. Configure the shared
functions from the V3 Master Architecture and Module 7 specification. The addendum is not permission to skip
work or approvals.
2. Shared mandatory company controls
 • GOV-01 Governance: The owner retains reserved strategic powers and protected oversight, while the
 employed General Manager (GM) holds company-wide operational accountability. Owner access to reporting
does not authorize routine operational interference.
• GOV-02 Delegation: Department heads, Project Managers and designated officers may authorize decisions
 only within documented project, function, category, value and risk limits; above-limit or exceptional decisions
go to GM. No employee may approve their own originating action.
• GOV-03 Technical independence: Valid safety and professional certifications are performed by competent
 authorized persons; neither a GM nor a financial waiver can substitute for a mandatory technical/statutory
clearance.
• GOV-04 Evidence: Worker, junior designer, client, storekeeper, inspector, supplier and auditor original
 submissions are preserved with authorship, timestamp, linked versions and tamper-evident history. Review
adds new records; it does not overwrite originals.
• GOV-05 Segregation: Requests, verification, approval, payment, receipt and audit must be appropriately
 independent. Where team size makes perfect separation impractical, a documented compensating review by
an independent qualified officer is mandatory.
• GOV-06 Client access: Client portal content is limited to that client/project and expressly published revision;
 source design files, worker complaints, confidential pay, internal contracts and audit cases remain restricted.

04 Shared assignment, task and review engine
• Every technical task must have project, discipline, package revision, assignee, originating instruction,
planned deadline, submission, reviewer and status.
• System supports draft, submitted for review, returned with comments, technically approved, released and
superseded states; approval and release are separately logged.
• The reviewer may create numbered correction comments; the author submits a new working version; earlier
versions remain accessible to appropriate staff.
• Management controls overall design/project target; department leads allocate own junior tasks and set
subordinate deadlines.
• Individual professionals have attributable submission histories and task-quality/turnaround records.
Department leads cannot silently erase or alter junior-originated work evidence; management retains visibility.
• Management can assess employee performance using timely reporting, completeness, verified technical
quality and rework, with context for client changes or missing inputs.

08 Mechanical and plumbing submodule
• Upon structural release, the Lead Mechanical Engineer receives the compatible architectural and structural
source references.
• Lead delegates plumbing/water supply, drainage, sanitary fittings, equipment, ventilation/mechanical
systems as relevant to contracted scope and project type.
• Junior deliverables may include PDFs, working files, system sizing, schedules and inspection-ready drawings
where required; exact content remains configurable.
• Lead records discipline checks, clash/penetration coordination, corrections and a final technical package
release to Electrical.
• Changes to structure/architecture must create cross-discipline coordination issues and revalidate dependent
cost items.
09 Electrical submodule
• Lead Electrical Engineer receives the latest compatible approved Architecture, Structural and
Mechanical/Plumbing packages.
• Tasks may cover lighting, power, load distribution, circuits, low-current services, equipment points and
relevant schedules in accordance with scope.
• Lead reviews technical design, electrical safety matters, coordination with plumbing and structural elements
and version completeness.
• Approved Electrical release is made available to QS; any corrections to upstream disciplines trigger
controlled dependent revisions.
10 Quantity surveying and budgeting submodule
• Lead QS takes control only after required four discipline package approvals and assigns quantity takeoff and
estimating tasks to estimators.
• Quantities must reference exact approved drawing revisions, units, measurement assumptions and scope
exclusions; inconsistencies become coordination questions.
• Prepare both summary budget and detailed budget. Lead QS gives technical approval before Module 3
publication consideration.
• Detailed budget remains permission controlled; client-facing summary or detailed view is an explicit
management publishing choice in Module 3.
• Changes with measurable cost consequences require re-estimation; preserve pricing assumptions, source
date and estimate version.
• If a client only contracts selected design services, Module 2 still records the appropriate deliverable package
and honest exclusions, without implying every discipline was commissioned.

13 Shared rules carried across all six modules
 CROSS-MODULE RULE
IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENT
One lifecycle record
Maintain one project ID from confirmed site-visit booking
through design, approvals, contracts, construction and
post-handover. Never create a disconnected project just to
open a new module.
Role separation
User role, project assignment and explicit approval authority
determine every permitted action. Technical sign-off, financial
validation, management approval and client acceptance are
distinct records.
Evidence integrity
Preserve original submissions, attachments, timestamps,
authors and immutable decision histories. Corrections create
new records or versions; no silent overwriting.
Selective client access
Client accounts can access only their own projects and
explicitly authorized files or updates. Internal working/source
files, staff performance data and financial notes are never
published by default.
Conditional service scope
Clients may purchase design only, printing only, approvals,
technical supervision, labour-only building or full construction.
Modules not contracted are not mandatory project steps.
Management-controlled exceptions
Any permitted exception requires identified management
authorization, reason, date and retained evidence. Mandatory
legal/technical clearances cannot be overridden.
Security and audit
Require authenticated accounts, least privilege, encrypted
transport/storage, file scanning, access logs, project-scoped
permissions, backups and recoverability.

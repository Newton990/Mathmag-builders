Design control
Module 7 is a continuous company-wide governance and enabling-services module. It operates alongside all 
Modules 1-6 and is not a seventh chronological project step. Its GM delegation and owner-reserved decisions 
override generic references to “Management” in older module documentation. User-approved technical and 
financial gates remain binding.
Previously selected Module 7 business rules below are retained without changing the individual approved policy 
choices. All legal and numerical thresholds require configured validation.

03  Module 7 — Central Administration
Module 7 is the company-wide control layer across all modules and projects. It contains Governance and 
Delegation, HR, Finance/Accounts, Compliance, Central QA, Independent Audit and Security/Administration. The 
selected design deliberately separates daily GM-led operations from owner-reserved governance and professionally 
independent sign-off.
A. Governance and administrative leadership
M7-G01  Owner vs General Manager
SELECTED RULE — Employed GM controls routine operations within a standing owner-approved delegation 
framework (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Owner receives cross-company audit and risk dashboards, governance proposals 
and matters explicitly reserved under governing instruments and approved delegation. No per-invoice, hiring, site 
or standard purchasing intervention.
M7-G02  Department decisions and escalation
SELECTED RULE — Delegate HR, procurement, finance and project approvals to appropriate qualified heads 
within risk, project and spending matrices (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Enforce approval authority based on action type, location, source budget, 
cumulative thresholds, absence status and conflict-of-interest; no purchase splitting, retroactive self-approval or 
professional safety override.
M7-G03  GM absence and deputy succession
SELECTED RULE — Preapproved qualified acting deputies activated through HR under documented limits without 
fresh owner signoff for each absence (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: GM nominates succession, owner preapproves standing framework; HR verifies 
triggering condition, time-limited access and nonconflicted eligibility; owner receives notice, not routine decision 
queue.
M7-G04  Emergency management quorum
SELECTED RULE — If no eligible acting deputy, preapproved Emergency Management Committee exercises 
collective restricted authority (confirmed policy).
Required control and evidence: HR activation, recorded attendance/quorum/recusals/votes, heightened consensus 
for elevated risk, time-bound scope, no owner-reserved decision powers, protected audit and owner notifications.
M7-G05  Management dashboard and meeting decisions
SELECTED RULE — Single accountable company task/decision register with cross-project performance and closure 
verification (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: GM approves department plans/KPIs, management tasks and action owners; 
deadlines, evidence and independent report-back; elevated exceptions visible to governance. Meeting notes cannot 
substitute for transactional authorization.

B. Human Resources and employee administration
M7-H01  Staffing plan and recruitment
SELECTED RULE — Head proposes vacancy; HR verifies approved position/budget, credentials and fair selection; 
delegated management appoints (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Track requisition, conflict disclosures, advert/referrals, structured shortlist, 
interviews and hiring decisions; protect applicant privacy; material/senior roles require higher delegated 
authorization.
M7-H02  Employee onboarding and access
SELECTED RULE — HR verifies identity, contract and role, work eligibility and any professional licences; role-
specific training and portal activation (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Signed employment agreement, pay terms, statutory enrolment where applicable, 
emergency contacts with privacy controls, induction, PPE, supervisor and permitted sites; IT enables least privilege 
only after HR/authorized approval.
M7-H03  Attendance, overtime and payroll input
SELECTED RULE — Employee-original attendance plus independent supervisor verification and separate payroll 
calculation/approval (existing; carried forward).
Required control and evidence: No overwrite of check-in/out timestamps; corrections separately requested and 
approved. Overtime requires explicit authorized policy/contract approval; evaluate labour law and entitlement. 
Payroll must not assume subcontractor workers are direct employees.
M7-H04  Leave, shift, absence and replacement
SELECTED RULE — Employee-initiated requests and documented operational cover with HR entitlement checking 
and delegated approval (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Annual/medical/emergency leave types, dates, supporting sensitive records with 
strict access, staffing conflict checks, holiday/overtime rules via verified local law, balanced leave ledger, escalating 
nonconflicted review for disputes.
M7-H05  Performance reviews and development
SELECTED RULE — Role-specific evidence-based reviews, worker and manager responses, appeal route and 
improvement plans (confirmed principle, selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Compare to assigned role and actual constraints; original work reports/verified 
output, quality and safety compliance, fair context (late materials, weather, design changes) and non-retaliation; 
performance score must not silently trigger wage deduction/discipline.
M7-H06  Training, trade competency and credential expiry
SELECTED RULE — HR central training record with independent qualified verification and equipment/professional 
role restrictions (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Mandatory training matrix per role/site, renewal reminders, training 
booking/attendance evidence, qualified signoff; expired mandatory licences block affected professional/operation 
activities only, not every unrelated task.

M7-H07  Complaints, grievances and discipline
SELECTED RULE — Confidential HR cases, fair fact-finding, opportunity to respond, independent appeal and 
protected channels for GM/supervisor cases (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Role-restricted evidence, due process per contracts/applicable Kenyan law, non-
retaliation against reporters, no automatic penalties, independent auditor oversight for fraud/retaliation/implicated 
management, protected appeal separation.
M7-H08  Employment changes and offboarding
SELECTED RULE — Documented contract variation, role transfer or exit; immediate risk-based access/asset 
recovery, final lawful balances and records (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: HR obtains authorization, tracks notices and legally required benefits, 
projects/worker assignments, PPE/assets, Accounts final payroll/advance settlement under law; revoke access and 
preserve original worker evidence even after departure.
M7-H09  Payroll and worker advances
SELECTED RULE — Accounts independently calculates employee pay and verified recoveries, with separated 
delegated pay approval and payer controls (previously confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Supports daily/weekly/monthly/piecework/fixed-task; employee visibility limited to 
own terms and statements; any pay advance/deduction governed by actual agreement and law; errors corrected 
transparently with versioned ledger.
C. Finance, budgeting and commercial controls
M7-F01  Chart of accounts and project finance
SELECTED RULE — Central company general ledger with project/job/cost-code subledgers and distinct commercial 
commitments (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Separate project quoted budget, approved changes, PO commitment, accrued 
expense, actual expense, cash paid, receivable and recoverable advance; maintain multi-contract scope, tax 
categorization and traceable cost center dimensions.
M7-F02  Budget approvals and variance management
SELECTED RULE — Management authorizes initial budget and material revisions; QS forecasts and Accounts 
validates actual finance (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: PM/QS plan cost-to-complete, requisitions checked against budget commitment and 
delegated thresholds; supported variation vs internal forecast distinguished; exceptions not silently added to client 
contract price.
M7-F03  Client billing and debt collection
SELECTED RULE — Contract-specific milestone/schedule/verified-progress invoices with Accounts reconciliation 
and management-controlled credit decisions (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: PM progress certificate and QS valuation when applicable; Accounts issues 
controlled invoice and posts independently verified payment. Financial reminder sending follows previous rule: 
Accounts manually controls timing, though overdue risk alerts may be automatic.

M7-F04  Supplier payable control
SELECTED RULE — Approved supplier/master verification; valid award and PO, accepted GRN or contract-
permitted advance stage, verified invoice and payment destination (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Prevent duplicates and invoice splitting; account changes require out-of-band 
verification and renewed approval of affected pending payments. Sensitive supplier bank details not exposed to 
ordinary site users.
M7-F05  Supplier advances, shortage claims and recoveries
SELECTED RULE — Contract-permitted risk-screened prepayment with formal milestone offset and monitored 
recovery (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Track supplier default/notice, alternative sourcing, Contracts enforceable remedies, 
Accounts exposure, settlement vs accounting impairment, delegated financial approval and independent audit; 
apply precise payment/recovery rights from signed documents.
M7-F06  Payment batch creation and disbursement
SELECTED RULE — Independent payable preparation, verification, authorization, secure payment initiation and 
separate post-payment reconciliation (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Maker/checker/approver/payer rights cannot collapse into one person; payment file 
integrity, approved payee crosscheck just before release, duplicate detection, failed/reversed payments, transfer 
fees and bank references. No pay without valid verified destination.
M7-F07  Petty cash, travel, expenses and staff imprests
SELECTED RULE — Controlled imprest/cashless-first approach with receipts, accountable limits, reimbursement 
review and timely settlement (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Employee claim or authorized cash advance, manager verifies business purpose, 
Accounts checks policy/duplicate receipts and budget, delegated approval, reconciliation, repayment/approved 
lawful recovery of unsupported spending; review recurring patterns.
M7-F08  Bank, mobile-money and cash reconciliation
SELECTED RULE — Accounts performs scheduled independent bank/M-Pesa reconciliation with investigation and 
controlled journal corrections (new selected Option B).
Required control and evidence: Import statements/read-only direct reconciliation where supported, map 
receipts/payments and fees, monitor unpresented/failed/duplicate transfers, require second-role signoff for 
reconciliation and material journals, audit unmatched transactions.
M7-F09  Payroll authorization and statutory remittances
SELECTED RULE — HR controls authorized staff/terms; site verifies work; Accounts independently calculates and 
pays only authorized net pay (confirmed pattern, finalized Option B).
Required control and evidence: No payroll for unverified or duplicate worker records; applicable 
PAYE/pension/social-security and other payroll obligations configured from current rules; bank-beneficiary 
changes under same strong out-of-band safeguards.
M7-F10  Tax/statutory filing and professional fees
SELECTED RULE — Central Compliance owns requirements calendar while Accounts prepares financial 
returns/payments and appropriately authorized professionals certify (new selected Option B).

D. Compliance and central quality assurance
M7-C01  Statutory/professional obligations register
SELECTED RULE — Central qualified Compliance Officer maintains source-linked register of obligations, proof and 
renewal dates (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Qualified Leads own discipline requirements; Approvals Officer/PM run project 
submissions; automatic reminders, risk escalation and affected-activity restrictions where mandatory prerequisites 
missing; management cannot self-issue technical clearance.
M7-C02  Independent compliance continuity
SELECTED RULE — GM appoints qualified Compliance Officer; predesignates competent deputies for HR 
activation; last-resort external backup preapproved (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Acting rights time-bounded, scoped and independently audited; external backup 
must receive least-privilege access and confidentiality terms, without requiring per-absence owner signoff.
M7-C03  Regulatory change control
SELECTED RULE — Compliance monitors trusted official changes, qualified professionals assess applicability and 
effective date; PM/QS/Contracts assess each affected project (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Do not assume retroactivity. Mandatory affected work may be restricted; client 
variations negotiated where lawful, no unilateral charges. Unlock only after professional/safety/compliance 
clearance and authorized digital work release.

M7-C04  Quality oversight and hold points
SELECTED RULE — Independent Central QA register for recurring construction defects, lot issues, 
nonconformances, investigations and preventive actions (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: QA may impose narrow provisional serious-defect holds but cannot replace 
licensed professional signoff. Independent qualified lead confirms/resolves hold; PM implements, QS/Accounts 
track costs and source of legal liability.
M7-C05  Supplier and material quality lot tracking
SELECTED RULE — QA tracks supplier lots from PO/GRN through stores, transfer and installation; unsafe or 
suspect lots quarantined (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Storekeeper physically isolates and blocks issuance; professionals test/clear or 
reject; Procurement pursues supplier remedies; affected installed work gets scoped professional assessment; 
Accounts adjusts credited cost only against verified documents.
M7-C06  Corrective action verification and recurrence
SELECTED RULE — Every defect/incident creates accountable root-cause review and measurable 
corrective/preventive actions (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Technical Leads approve technical measures; independent competent verifier tests 
effectiveness; QA trends recurrence across sites; protected auditors verify high-risk financial/governance actions 
are truly closed.
E. Independent audit, ethics and investigations
M7-A01  Independent auditor appointment and protection
SELECTED RULE — Lead independent auditor has owner/governance-controlled appointment/removal, separate 
from unilateral GM operational control (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: HR administers terms/resources, GM ensures cooperation, auditor retains protected 
read access and secured case evidence; no auditor may approve company spending they later audit as independent.
M7-A02  Automated anomaly and risk analytics
SELECTED RULE — Risk-based monitoring for duplicate invoices, split purchases, beneficiary changes, suspicious 
journal entries and material diversion (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Alerts preserve allegation/evidence status, not guilt; compare cross-project 
procurement, material discrepancies, payroll and supplier master records; prioritize serious cases without 
assuming a flag proves fraud.
M7-A03  Anonymous/identified whistleblower channel
SELECTED RULE — Protected confidential or anonymous intake with case reference, status updates and 
independent triage (confirmed Option B).
Required control and evidence: Separate GM-related cases from GM; restrict reporter identity and no automatic 
inference linking anonymous cases to staff identities. Anti-retaliation monitoring for known protected reporters 
without disclosure; independent appeal review.
M7-A04  Finding resolution and independent closure
SELECTED RULE — Auditor records severity, findings/evidence; nonconflicted management assigns corrective 
owners, independent auditor verifies closure (confirmed Option B).

Required control and evidence: HR governs activation/deactivation; IT enforces least privilege; emergency time-

SELECTED RULE — Event-triggered task notifications for compliance, safety, inspections, overdue invoices and

SELECTED RULE — Encrypted protected file storage, backups, disaster recovery drills, audit retention and secure

approval queue. GM: delivery, budgets, resources, exceptions. PM: site operations; Accounts: finance; HR: protected

7.1 Cross-module authority interpretation
 GOV-01 Governance: The owner retains reserved strategic powers and protected oversight, while the employed 
General Manager (GM) holds company-wide operational accountability. Owner access to reporting does not 
authorize routine operational interference.
 GOV-02 Delegation: Department heads, Project Managers and designated officers may authorize decisions only 
within documented project, function, category, value and risk limits; above-limit or exceptional decisions go to 
GM. No employee may approve their own originating action.
 GOV-03 Technical independence: Valid safety and professional certifications are performed by competent 
authorized persons; neither a GM nor a financial waiver can substitute for a mandatory technical/statutory 
clearance.
 GOV-04 Evidence: Worker, junior designer, client, storekeeper, inspector, supplier and auditor original 
submissions are preserved with authorship, timestamp, linked versions and tamper-evident history. Review 
adds new records; it does not overwrite originals.
 GOV-05 Segregation: Requests, verification, approval, payment, receipt and audit must be appropriately 
independent. Where team size makes perfect separation impractical, a documented compensating review by 
an independent qualified officer is mandatory.
 GOV-06 Client access: Client portal content is limited to that client/project and expressly published revision; 
source design files, worker complaints, confidential pay, internal contracts and audit cases remain restricted.
 GOV-07 Controls: Accounts verifies funds/beneficiaries and books actual transactions; QS values cost and 
forecasts; Contracts verifies commercial entitlements; Compliance oversees obligations; QA checks systemic 
quality; independent audit observes, investigates and independently verifies closure.
 GOV-08 Exceptions and continuity: Documented financial/administrative exceptions may be decided by the 
authorized delegated manager or GM; there is no exception to mandatory law, safety, professional status or an 
already-required client acceptance. GM absence invokes only preapproved and HR-activated acting authority.
7.2 Management and audit dashboard expectations
 GM operational inbox: delegated approvals, department performance, overdue controls, restricted equipment, 
financial exposure, statutory gates and incident response.
 Owner oversight: high-level project margins/cash exposure, protected independent audit results, GM 
performance, strategic risks and owner-reserved resolutions; no routine payment approval queue.
 Department heads: staff workload, qualification expiries, pending reviews and disciplinary access only within 
legal and role constraints.
 Independent auditor: protected access to original immutable records, anomalies and case management, with 
management-involved cases escalated to non-conflicted owner/governance.
 System administrators operate infrastructure and permissions under dual-control but cannot impersonate 
another staff member or silently change audit evidence.
7.3 Implementation and assurance
The full audit and functional testing matrix is documented in the Master Architecture and Integration Testing 
Guide. Modules 1-6 point to Module 7 policies for roles, finance, HR, records, security and audit. All client project 
routes remain configurable by contracted service, not by forcing all seven stages sequentially.
